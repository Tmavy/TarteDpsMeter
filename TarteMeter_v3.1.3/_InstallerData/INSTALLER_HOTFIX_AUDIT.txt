TarteMeter v3.1.3 — Installer Audit

Retained fixes
- Browse, Install/Update, Remove and Launch normalize the same absolute DSClient-Win64-Shipping.exe path.
- PowerShell hashtable splatting is used instead of fragile blank-line continuations.
- VBS launchers are ASCII without BOM and use strict CRLF.
- Paths with spaces, OneDrive and Cyrillic characters remain quoted.

Release verification
- Setup expects version 3.1.3.
- Setup verifies the runtime marker multi_target_aoe_owner=true.
- Compact mutex: Local\TarteMeterOverlay_v313.
- Settings file: window_settings_v14_compact.ini.
- Installed history and archived timelines are preserved.

UE4SS note
- Repair UE4SS restores the bundled d935b5b core. Do not use Repair during the co-op A/B test, because it invalidates the comparison.
