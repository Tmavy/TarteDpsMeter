TarteMeter v3.1.3 - Compact AoE Source Ownership

MAIN FIX
- Fixed multi-target AoE damage leaking to the character selected after a swap.
- A concrete LastAttacker or verified projectile/source now seeds a short, bounded multi-target burst owner.
- Same-value callbacks from nearby targets in the same 180 ms burst inherit that verified owner only when exactly one owner is possible.
- Active-character fallback never seeds or extends the burst cache.
- Exact Chako, Theresa, Roxy, Dana, and other source matches still outrank burst propagation.
- Conflicting verified owners disable propagation instead of guessing.

OBSERVED TEST CASE
The supplied timeline contained duplicate AoE values:
- Theresa 8369 and Dana 8369 at 12.829
- Theresa 10607 at 12.870 and Dana 10607 at 12.947
v3.1.3 keeps both targets in the verified Theresa burst after the swap.

PERFORMANCE
- No new hooks.
- No additional FindAllOf calls or world scans.
- Existing single snapshot per required batch is reused.
- Burst cache contains plain strings, numbers and booleans only.
- Cache is limited to 256 entries and expires after 180 ms.
- Cache is cleared on Reset.

UNCHANGED
- Two verified combat hooks.
- Damage/DPS/Crit/Hits/Max/Avg formulas.
- Chako separate row and source recognition.
- F6 save, History, Timeline and manual Reset.
- Compact UI and installer path hotfix.
