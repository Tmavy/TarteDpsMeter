TarteMeter v3.1.3 — Runtime and Crash-Safety Audit

AoE correction
- Source ownership can propagate across multiple enemy actors only after authoritative source evidence.
- Active-character fallback cannot create a multi-target owner record.
- Conflicting owners suppress propagation.
- Time, distance and capacity limits prevent a stale attack from becoming a global owner.

Safety invariants
- Exactly two combat hook paths.
- One RegisterHook wrapper call site.
- Exactly one FindAllOf("DsMonsterCharacter") call site.
- No persistent UObject in deferred queues or ownership caches.
- One conditional monster snapshot per required batch.
- Bounded queues and caches.
- Reset clears the new AoE cache.
- No automatic reset after save.

No network hooks were added. TarteMeter does not hook PostLogin, RestartPlayer, possession, pawn replication or controller replication.
