TARTEMETER v2.7.4 - RESET AND CRASH-SAFETY AUDIT

SOURCE LOG FINDINGS
- The supplied dps_meter(7).txt identifies itself as TarteMeter v2.6.2, not v2.7.3.
- That old runtime contains SESSION_AUTO_SAVED followed by AUTO_RESET_AFTER_SAVE.
- 321 AUTO_RESET_AFTER_SAVE entries were counted in the supplied log.
- No HOOK_FAIL, hook callback error, background loop error, damage commit error, or game-thread batch error was found in the supplied logs.
- The observed boss/hazard actor-ID change was coincidental; v2.6.2 reset because its 10-second idle auto-save boundary was followed by another damage event.

V2.7.4 RESET POLICY
- Enemy actor IDs and displayed enemy names are metadata only.
- A boss phase actor, add, or fire tornado cannot call clear_values().
- clear_values() requires a private token and is called only by reset_meter().
- Idle detection writes latest JSON/CSV checkpoints and never clears damage.
- Manual RESET is the only normal clearing path.

V2.7.4 CRASH-SAFETY CHANGES
- Process-wide runtime guard blocks duplicate Lua initialization.
- Setup removes TarteMeter's local enabled.txt and relies on one mods.txt entry.
- Setup disables enabled.txt markers in known legacy DPS mod folders.
- Setup verifies exact SHA-256 hashes of installed main.lua and DPSWindow.ps1.
- Stale delayed batches skip FindAllOf during possible world loading or teardown.
- One transient monster snapshot is capped at 1024 enemy actors.
- Timeline, source, damage, target-attribution, and history containers are bounded.
- Enemy display names use class/object names instead of speculative property probing.
- Only the two previously verified combat hooks remain.
- No UObject is intentionally retained in long-lived queues or caches.

LIMITS OF THIS AUDIT
- Static validation cannot prove that UE4SS itself will never crash inside native engine code.
- No crash dump was supplied for this incident.
- Final safety must be confirmed in-game on the current DragonSword build and UE4SS v3.0.1.
